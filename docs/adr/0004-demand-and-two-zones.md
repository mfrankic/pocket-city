# City-wide demand, road access, two zones

A zoned plot grows only if it touches a road (cardinal neighbors) and that zone’s city-wide demand is positive. Demand is two numbers for the whole city, not a search for nearby jobs. Industrial is not a zone: without pollution or freight it is a second Commercial. Commercial is the jobs zone; Residential is the population zone.
