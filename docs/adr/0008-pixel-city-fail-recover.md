# PIXEL CITY is fail and recover, not a small toy

The finished game is 64×64, generated terrain, pan/zoom, overlays, and month-bucket graphs. The player paints roads and zones and stamps seven facilities from the start (Station, Tower, Park, School, Police, Firehouse, Hospital); money is the gate, not unlocks. There is no win and no agents. The city fails by husks and jammed roads; recovery is the game. We rejected stopping at the two-zone toy because that was only ChatGPT’s first milestone, and we rejected a population score and ChatGPT’s unlock tree because fail/recover is the loop. The share stays an open catalog for later systems, not a win screen.
