# Three city-wide demands; levels on a frozen footprint

Demand stays city-wide with cardinal road access, not a search for nearby jobs. Residential is base + all jobs − population (the base remains so 0/0 can start). Commercial is population − shop jobs. Industrial is a zone: shop jobs − factory jobs, so factories are not a second Commercial. Grown buildings have level 1–3 on the frozen rectangle; stats are base × level × plots. A house needs School coverage for level 2 and Hospital coverage for level 3. Tax is one player-set city-wide rate; income is tax times population. We rejected local demand (pathfinding) and three tax sliders.
