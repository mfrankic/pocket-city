# Health bands; dribble births and levels

Each tick grows at most one new house, shop, and factory (complete, level 1) and at most one level-up per those kinds. Eligible new footprints are 1×1 plots or 2×2 rectangles with demand, access, and land value. Abandoned buildings never level. Health is 0–1 per building; struggling still produces; abandoned is the bottom band (not only zero) so a husk is not a one-tick flicker. Happiness is the average of health, HUD only, not a fifth nibble. We kept dribble so the city does not flash into existence, and we rejected “buildings do not rot” because fail/recover needs a husk you can see.
