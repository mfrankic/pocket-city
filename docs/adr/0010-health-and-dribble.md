# Health bands; dribble births and levels

Each tick grows at most one new house, shop, and factory into Construction, then at most one level-up per those kinds. Construction lasts one Month of ticks from birth (not aligned to the calendar month), then becomes level 1 with health 1. Level-up stays instant. An outage does not freeze the timer. Eligible new footprints are 1×1 plots or 2×2 rectangles with demand, access, and land value. Abandoned buildings never level; Construction never levels. Construction produces nothing and has no health; fire intensity may sit on its lots but does not nibble until health exists; facilities skip Construction. Health is 0–1 per finished building; struggling still produces; abandoned is the bottom band (not only zero) so a husk is not a one-tick flicker. Happiness is the average of health on finished buildings, HUD only, not a fifth nibble. We kept dribble so the city does not flash into existence, rejected score-roll growth, and rejected replacing health with per-building happiness.
