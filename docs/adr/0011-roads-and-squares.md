# Power follows roads; coverage is a square

Power and water flood-fill along connected roads, with capacity in plots. Lake is unbuildable terrain; water is the utility flag; a Tower must touch a lake and needs power; a Station needs nothing. Park, School, Police, Firehouse, and Hospital use a square of lots (through roads); unpowered coverage is none except Park. Pollution, fire, and crime are per-plot numbers; traffic is per road component. Fire is intensity on lots with low cardinal spread, not an agent; each month may ignite or cause an outage. We rejected a second wire/pipe lot kind, radius power, and one graph for both pipes and parks.
