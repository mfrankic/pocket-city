# The ChatGPT share is a catalog, not a spec

The originating ChatGPT conversation stays an open catalog for later systems (garbage, extra events, more kinds). It is not a backlog to implement in order, and it does not override ADR-0003, 0007, 0008, or 0011. From that share we are taking Construction, Maintenance, irregular stamps, lot inspect, sim speed, and power/water on the HUD — not unlocks, a win, three taxes, radius coverage, or Small/Large roads.
