# Unemployment does not nibble while a Shop is not producing

Unemployment still nibbles House health when finished Shops exist and jobs are too few. It does not nibble while a Shop is in Construction or Abandoned: that gap is the scaffold/husk, not a player who refused to zone Commercial. We rejected shorter Construction, weaker Outage, and more starting Money; those either fight Fail/recover or only delay a husk loop.
