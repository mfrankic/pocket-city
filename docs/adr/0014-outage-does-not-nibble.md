# Outage darkens the city; it does not husk it

An Outage still cuts Power (and Water, because Towers need Power): no new growth on dark plots, civic coverage off, HUD percents drop. Missing Power and Water do not nibble Health during that month. A bulldozed Station still nibbles. One nibble per tick still Abandoned a House in a Month (16 × 0.05), so lowering the nibble count was not enough. We kept Fail/recover on Fire, unemployment (when Shops are producing), traffic, and a real utility hole.
